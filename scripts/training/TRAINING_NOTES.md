# Kirigami Studio — Training & Evaluation Journal

This journal tracks the quality verification, failure diagnostics, and curation of figures generated by the FLUX.2 pipeline, serving as the ground-truth dataset preparation log for subsequent LoRA fine-tuning.

---

## 🎯 Evaluation Criteria (Scale 1 to 5)

Every generated 3x2 alternatives sheet and individual pick (especially default Pick #3) is evaluated against strict physical and stylistic benchmarks:

1. **Complete Object (Zero Busts)**: Is the subject 100% visible from end to end (e.g. nose to tail and all paws for animals, nose to tailfin and wingtips for airplanes, wheels and chassis for vehicles), centered with generous margin? (`Pass` / `Fail`)
2. **Isometric Three-Quarter Perspective**: Does the view capture top, front, and side simultaneously with true 3D spatial depth? (`Pass` / `Fail`)
3. **Clean Planar Facets (Zero Crease Lines)**: Is the model rendered with solid matte cardstock facets and flat directional shading, with ABSOLUTELY ZERO black wireframe lines, fold crease markings, or blueprint seams? (`Pass` / `Fail`)
4. **Reference Style Fidelity**: Does the figure match the reference color palette, markings, proportions, and character? (`Score 1-5`)
5. **LoRA Dataset Approval**: Only samples achieving a Score $\ge 4$ with zero structural defects are approved and appended to `lora_dataset.jsonl`.
